## 3. Auto-processing of EPFO claims at 71% of total claims processed after CITES 2.0

- Ministry: Ministry of Labour & Employment
- Previous: 39% auto-processing; rejection ratio 23%; average claims settlement time 11.15 days
- Takeaway: After CITES 2.0, auto-processing of claims rose from 39% to 71%, the rejection ratio fell from 23% to 7.82%, and settlement time fell from 11.15 days to 2.57 days.
- Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseDetail.aspx?PRID=2338667

Draft tweet:

> 71%: share of EPFO claims auto-processed after CITES 2.0, up from 39%. The overall rejection ratio fell from 23% to 7.82%. Average claims settlement time declined from 11.15 days to 2.57 days, the Central Board of Trustees was told at its 240th meeting. #EPFO
